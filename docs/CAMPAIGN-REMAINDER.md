# Remaining campaign: gameplay and scene treatment

M28–M30 and SM04–SM06 now have first-pass gameplay scripts. M31–M70 and SM07–SM09 below are fleshed-out implementation plans, not playable scripts. Their dialogue already exists. A mission becomes selectable only after its actors, objectives, failure paths and map start are implemented.

Every chapter follows intent → approach → complication → response → extraction → consequence. The initial line explains what the crew needs and why. Each stage must name the responsible character, the action, its target/marker and any interaction button or constraint. Remote conversations remain radio/phone calls at the actors' real positions. Nearby conversations use local framing. A scene may observe a scripted movement but must never pull all three actors to one spot just for a conversation.

All initial implementations use full mission retry. A future checkpoint must reconstruct cargo, vehicles, survivors, timers, hostility, active character and all completed interactions before advertising checkpoint restart.

## Recommended build sequence

1. Playtest and survey the new desert block, including actual tanker coupling and route clearance.
2. Implement M31–M35 and the rescue/escort consequences; attach meaningful garage rewards.
3. Build a reusable offshore staging area and prototype M42's drop before enabling M36–M48 as a complete chain.
4. Prototype M55's timed three-site switching and the tower interior routes before implementing the financial assault.
5. Prototype the final convoy, aircraft boarding and marine fallback before committing to M62–M70's camera script.

The bibles are creative source material. Their proposed geometry and vehicle capabilities are not evidence that those assets exist in the installed game. Any necessary substitution must be reflected in dialogue, objectives, markers and this plan together.
