## Changes

- **M07 relay:** creates a frozen satellite-dish prop beside the surveyed antenna interaction point. Ice plays a sustained equipment-work animation during the seven-second interaction. The existing roof and Maze Bank surveys remain authoritative.
- **M07 helicopter:** starts the rotor and seats a live pilot synchronously, clear of the rotor. Holds the response safely until the receiver scene finishes, then releases it to attack. The reveal follows the aircraft instead of a potentially dead pilot. Protection ends at the reveal.
- **M07 parachute:** a visible bag lies west of the dish. Walking within two meters as Ice gives and verifies the parachute, then removes the bag. No context press or native pickup streaming is required; another brother cannot consume it.
- **M07 pickup:** sedan doors are unlocked. Normal F / controller Y entry remains available; E / D-pad Right beside the stopped car also requests a free rear seat using a normal boarding task. The objective waits for actual occupancy.
- **Shared station behavior:** never give the active player an NPC guard task at deployment. Release an old on-foot station task when that hero takes control. A seated companion holding position keeps the seat instead of receiving an on-foot guard order after switching.
- **M08 CCTV:** replaces the empty platform interaction with an exterior cabinet beside a visible port security cabin, on a checked level apron. A clear prompt identifies the cabinet and triggers a work animation. This is an exterior worksite, not a walkable cabin interior.
- **M08 assignments:** hold separate crew stations through the camera/sentry/loading work; release the hold for extraction and on teardown. Gohan's follow task is only assigned while he is an NPC and resumes after a player handoff.
- **M08 cargo:** no physical attachment to forklift forks. During the short scripted drive, a frozen, collision-disabled visual crate follows the forks. Transfer onto the flatbed disables collision before attaching and verifies the result before increasing the count. Missing or rejected cargo fails the required scene instead of advancing. Watching, skipping and cancellation have explicit end states. Guess keeps his driver seat. The response technical's crew now seats synchronously outside its collision body; the gunner uses the rear station.
- **KJ:** approaches from about 110 meters instead of 150, still off camera and at least 80 meters away. Delivery target speed rises from 20 to 36 m/s; traffic avoidance, a nearby road destination and three-second retasking help avoid stalls. He slows for the handover. The bounded roadside fallback is 45 seconds instead of 90. These are task settings, not a guaranteed arrival time.
- **Guess's ability:** engages in about a tenth of a real second. Raises peak and sliding grip together, narrows the slip angle and adds bounded sideways damping. Steering lock gets a smaller increase to avoid oversteer. No forward acceleration boost, heading snap or forced velocity assignment. The first airborne frame receives no added force. Handling restores when the ability ends or the driver changes.
