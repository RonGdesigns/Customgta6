# SM03, the mountain sprint, September 17, 2026

Ron played KJ's sprint on September 16 and reported two faults, then deferred them behind
the surveyor work: "a lot of it was a looping around and not a straight path to the
mountain," and "the AI wasn't a challenge." Both were real. Neither was the thing it looked
like, and both were measurable rather than matters of taste.

## What the route actually was

| | before | after |
|---|---|---|
| gates | 278 | 35 (19 on the road, 15 on the trail, the summit) |
| path | 18.97 km | 12.00 km |
| straight line start to summit | 7.27 km | 7.27 km |
| gates moving away from the finish | 69 | 0 |
| widest gap between gates | 124 m | 746 m on the road, 94 m on the trail |

The loop was not a feeling. The run crawled downtown surface streets rather than the freeway
KJ names, and it entered the Mount Chiliad zone **four separate times**, leaving twice to
visit Braddock Tunnel, Mount Gordo and Grapeseed before coming back. The mountain climb,
which is the point of the mission, was the last 8% of the gate list.

## How the corridor was rebuilt, without inventing geometry

Every one of those 278 keys was a real road position off the offline connected road graph.
So the new corridor is the **shortest chain through that same set of positions**: treat the
keys as nodes, join any pair within a shortcut radius, reject a join whose grade would be a
cliff face rather than a road, and take the shortest path. Where the old route passed near
itself, the shortcut edge exists and the loop is cut at that point. Nothing is authored that
was not already in the file.

The two legs are computed separately and for opposite reasons. The approach gets a 400 m
shortcut radius and 400 m gates, because it is a freeway sprint and the player should pick
his own line between markers. The climb gets a 60 m radius and 60 m gates, because
switchbacks need tight gates: a wide gate across a switchback is one the player reaches by
driving off the mountain.

The zone sequence now runs forward and only forward: Rancho, Strawberry, Vinewood, East
Vinewood, Vinewood Hills, Grand Senora Desert, San Chianski, Grapeseed, Mount Chiliad,
the Chiliad wilderness, the summit.

**11.9 km on real roads for 7.3 km of straight line is about as direct as that pair of
endpoints allows.** The remaining 1.6x is mountains, and a corridor down the west coast that
nobody has driven would be a worse answer than a measured one.

## The gates go onto real lanes at runtime

`Core/RaceRoute` is the M49 answer applied to a race. Roads are baked terrain, so no
placed-entity survey finds one; an authored gate is a **seed**, and the lane comes from the
nearest vehicle node when the game is running. Three rules it keeps, each of them a bug it
exists to refuse:

* **A trail gate is never snapped.** The nearest vehicle node to a point halfway up Chiliad
  is the road at the bottom of the mountain. A snap there does not correct the climb, it
  deletes it. This is the same distinction `MissionSites` draws when it grounds only `land`.
* **A snap may correct a gate; it may never move the race backward.** A node further from
  the finish than the gate before it would reintroduce the exact fault this route was
  rebuilt to remove, so that answer is refused and the seed kept.
* **Nothing here is a refusal.** No node means the seed is used and the count reported, the
  way `PlacementPreflight` only warns. The build line says how much of the route stands on a
  real lane, and it goes to the mission doctor as well as the log.

## The rivals

Their driving ability was already `1.0`, the engine's maximum, so there was nothing to
raise. **What made them harmless was the order**: a flat 39 m/s, about 87 mph, on a route
where the player's car runs to twice its stock redline because `WorldTuning` lifts every
ceiling in the world, theirs included. They were not driving badly. They were obeying a slow
instruction, in a fixed Elegy, while Ron arrived in whatever he had built.

Four changes:

* **They drive KJ's own cars, matched to the player's.** The first version of this handed
  both rivals the player's model, and Ron rejected it on September 19: "I definitely don't
  want them to have my car." `Core/RivalGrid` is KJ's roster of twelve base-game street cars
  spanning 41 to 52 m/s stock, and he fields the two whose **model** top speed sits closest
  to whatever the player turned up in, with that model excluded so a coincidence cannot put
  them in it either. They get the engine, transmission, brakes and springs fitted, so the
  comparison is model to model rather than his built car against two stock ones. A roster car
  that will not stream costs the grid its first choice, not a car: the next entry down is the
  next closest match by construction.
* **The commanded speed comes from the car's own capability**, read with
  `GET_VEHICLE_ESTIMATED_MAX_SPEED` so fitted parts count, at 94% of it on asphalt and 50% on
  the trail.
* **Aggressiveness to 1.0.**
* **`Core/RacePacing` keeps the race close at both ends**, bounded and logged: a rival more
  than 220 m behind is helped up to 1.35x over the next 400 m, one more than 320 m ahead
  eases back to 0.85x, and in between he simply drives. Both limits are capped and every
  correction is a log line, because an assist with no visible edge is indistinguishable from
  cheating.

**The correction moves the commanded speed and nothing else.** It never writes
`SET_VEHICLE_CHEAT_POWER_INCREASE` or the entity speed cap, which `WorldTuning` owns per
car: a second writer on those is the double-ownership bug this project keeps finding, and it
is not needed, because a car whose ceiling is already doubled can reach any speed this asks
for. A story test measures the distance between the two in the source.

**A rival is re-tasked on a changed gate or a genuine stall, never on a clock.** The pace is
trimmed with `SET_DRIVE_TASK_CRUISE_SPEED` in between, because re-issuing `DriveTo` restarts
the drive task and makes a driver hesitate. That is the rule `PreparationOperation.KeepDriving`
already keeps for crew drivers, for the same reason.

## Two brittle tests fixed on the way

A check written yesterday matched a literal newline in a source file, so it passed in one
worktree and failed in the next depending on line endings. It matches with the endings taken
out now. And the refusal test's fake answered one constant point for every seed, which ties
with the gate before it rather than falling behind it, so it proved nothing; it fails one
specific gate now.

## Live questions

Nobody has driven this. Whether the 400 m road gates read as a sprint or as vague
suggestions; whether `GET_CLOSEST_VEHICLE_NODE_WITH_HEADING` finds a sensible lane for all
nineteen road seeds, which the build line will say; whether two rivals in the player's own
car with the parts on are now too fast rather than too slow, which the assist and lift
numbers are there to be tuned by; and whether the fifteen-gate climb holds a racer on the
switchbacks. `Bloodlines.log` records the route report and every correction.
