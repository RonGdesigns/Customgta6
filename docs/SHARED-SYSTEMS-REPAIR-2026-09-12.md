# Shared systems, prologue and collision repair

This change builds on PR #40's LSIA car position. That position is preserved. It does not author the remaining unimplemented campaign chapters.

## Implemented changes

1. **Graphics native arguments:** vehicle shadows, vehicle LOD and ped LOD now receive a live entity and the intended value. Ownership follows the active player/car and restores defaults on release. The host releases the daytime grade during active scenes as intended. There are no native getters for the previous LOD/shadow settings; disable these features if another graphics mod should own them.
2. **Mission teammate handback:** switching into a role-track teammate invalidates their old AI task, so switching away reissues the current assignment. The active player remains under player control.
3. **Required scene outcomes:** the mission manager consumes the director's finished sequence even when the host never ticked the mission during the scene. Interrupted/failed required actions stop progression instead of allowing the next objective to run.
4. **Attempt state:** evidence, cargo custody, fleet upgrades and base flags are provisional until success. Mid-mission saves serialize their previous committed values. Abort/failure/teardown restore them; completed replays cannot overwrite later story outcomes. Existing ambiguous save data is retained, not guessed away.
5. **Full campaign reset:** the debug reset stops mission/prologue/scene activity and clears checkpoint, handoff and retry context. Crew capture runs before the save reset. Evidence, cargo and crew-van customization are also cleared.
6. **Clarity:** every M01–M30 and SM01–SM06 script now has target/reason/role/first-destination context. Long card text wraps. Retry recaps wait until gameplay resumes. M04 describes Guess's solo drive accurately. M18 explains the remaining solo gates and separate M19 launch. M26 explains lead plane → listen while second survives → second plane. The mission overlay names M27's actual Duster.
7. **Actual reward notices:** compare newly earned entitlements with the accepted attempt's baseline. Completed replays do not announce new cash or weapons. The continuous heist reports rewards from M19, M20 and M22; solo awards name only their owner. DLC weapons use catalog names. New base, fleet and housing access is announced.
8. **Current progression documentation:** all 33 weapon milestones and 36 playable payouts are generated by executing the C# reward rules in the test harness. The guide is freshness-checked. Historical harbor/planning notes are explicitly marked as superseded where appropriate.
9. **Side/roof deformation:** a nearby car's genuine collision and directional velocity change can add one bounded local dent. Left/right impacts affect the respective side; roof impacts affect the roof. Front/rear/underbody damage stays with GTA's existing collision behavior. No extra damage from merely resting overturned, teleports or long update gaps. At most 32 cars within 90m are sampled every 75ms, with 750ms between added dents per car. Despawned/reused handles are discarded. The existing health damage is preserved. `[VehicleDamage] PanelDamageEnabled` and `PanelDamageStrength` control this addition.
10. **Guess's prologue apartment:** the Mission Row street entrance now uses a separate GTA Online apartment shell instead of Franklin's Strawberry house. New room keys prevent old Franklin-room survey values from redirecting the scene. Entry uses the residence's streaming/probe settings and preserves the Guess ped. The call plays inside, then Guess walks to the exit and returns to his own street door before the later dockyard cut. If streaming fails, the existing controlled door fallback remains available.
11. **Phone animation lifetime:** calls stay raised through the dialogue, then request a natural lower and allow a short lowering beat. Skipping an unstarted call does not flash a phone. Cancellation releases an active call. On-foot radio scenes use the shared call behavior; actors in vehicles retain hands-free dialogue. The airport text check remains a separate short glance; the premature phone step at the outside home door is removed. The prologue's call text now reads as a conversation. A canceled call retries instead of silently completing the prologue.

## Verification and practical limits

Production compilation and the offline suites exercise real mod classes against GTA stand-ins. They verify state transitions, native argument shape, phone/scene lifetime, skip/abort behavior, reward idempotency and collision classification. They cannot certify streamed interior geometry, visual dent strength, controller feel or GTA's actual phone animation on each ped model. A build/install receipt records the final binaries and command results separately.

Suggested live test order:

1. Fresh/explicit prologue replay: drive from the preserved LSIA car spawn to Mission Row. Watch the home entry/call/exit; repeat once skipping the call. Confirm Guess stays Guess, phone remains raised while speaking, controls return, and no Strawberry exterior appears.
2. A radio scene with teammates far apart, then a scene in a car: positions and seats should remain intact; a driver should use radio rather than a hand-held phone.
3. Switch into and back out of a mission-assigned teammate during an approach. They should continue their assignment. Interrupt a required scene and retry; it must fail cleanly, not progress with missing actions.
4. Left and right side collisions, then a roof landing in a sedan and an SUV. Check visible dents, sensible health, no repeated crushing while stationary and no damage from a debug teleport.
5. M04's initial solo drive; M26's two-plane firing order. Finish a new reward mission and replay it: only the first completion should announce new rewards.

Apartment reference: [Bob74 GTAO apartment hi_1](https://github.com/Bob74/bob74_ipl/blob/master/gta_online/apartment_hi_1.lua). The room-center coordinate is a reference anchor, not an in-game survey of its door or furniture. Furniture interactions remain survey-gated.

Native references: [vehicle damage](https://github.com/citizenfx/natives/blob/master/VEHICLE/SetVehicleDamage.md), [collision normal](https://github.com/citizenfx/natives/blob/master/ENTITY/GetCollisionNormalOfLastHitForEntity.md), [phone task](https://github.com/citizenfx/natives/blob/master/TASK/TaskUseMobilePhone.md).
