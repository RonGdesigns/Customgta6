1. SOLO MISSIONS RATIONALE & STRUCTURAL INTEGRATION
PACING, INDIVIDUAL AGENCY & MECHANISTIC FOCUS
While the main 70-mission campaign focuses on high-octane trio coordination, these 9 dedicated Solo Missions (3 per protagonist across Acts I, II, and
III) provide narrative intimacy and deep individual gameplay loops:
Ice (Darius Vance):
 Focuses on black-market military arms logistics, gritty CQC takedowns, and settling painful wartime betrayal debts from his military
deployment years.
Gohan (Devin Mercer):
 Focuses on pure stealth infiltration, electronic warfare, physical server splicing, and exacting revenge on the white-collar auditors
who originally framed him.
Guess (Ron Ortiz):
 Focuses on illegal underground street racing, midnight drift contracts, hazardous off-road speed runs, and securing maritime escape
pipelines for the crew's family.
