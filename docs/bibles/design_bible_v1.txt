1. EXECUTIVE CONCEPT & NARRATIVE FOUNDATION
Los Santos: Bloodlines
 is an action-thriller narrative campaign built within the Grand Theft Auto V engine. The storyline
centers on three Black childhood friends from South Central Davis who left on athletic and academic scholarships fifteen
years prior. Having re-entered the criminal underworld through distinct, high-stakes trades, they inadvertently crash into one
another during an offshore wetwork operation at Terminal Island.
The design discards standard slow escort missions in favor of rapid, multi-vehicle combat operations featuring synchronized
character switching, high-altitude drops, sea-to-land breaches, and full-city pursuits.

4. 70-MISSION CAMPAIGN ARCHITECTURE
PART I: THE BLEEDING TRAIL
Missions 01 – 22
Setting up base in an abandoned Cypress Flats industrial shop, the trio works to clean biometrics, settle gang bounties, and eliminate
corrupt forensic detectives before their identities go live on federal wiretaps.
M06: "Clean Sweep" (First Dynamic Tri-Switch)
Heist on the Vespucci LSPD Evidence Depot. Gohan drops building power via access tunnels; Ice holds off tactical SWAT units in the
alleyway using heavy ordnance; Guess handles the armored getaway van timed to a passing freight train.
M10: "Open Throttle" (Combat Escort Gauntlet)
Escorting a modified engine transport across Del Perro Freeway at 95+ mph under relentless attack from cartel choppers and dirt bikes.
Ice repels gunships with an RPG, Gohan disables hostile ECUs via portable EMP, and Guess pits aggressive interceptors.
M19–M22: "The Port of Los Santos Heist"
Multi-phase major score involving Fort Zancudo Cargobobs, underwater container cutting, and high-speed maritime escape under Coast
Guard fire.
PART II: THE SQUEEZE
Missions 23 – 48
Driven out of Los Santos and into hiding across Blaine County. The crew discovers that Aegis Tactical and the Cifuentes Cartel are running
an illicit arms pipeline through the Alamo Sea basin.
M27: "Flight Risk" (Mid-Air Boarding Gauntlet)
Guess maneuvers a stunt plane directly over a chartered Shamal jet at 8,000 feet. Ice skydives onto the roof, cuts the cabin hatch with a
torch, and eliminates the cartel crew in freefall before dropping into the Pacific to Gohan’s speedboat.
M34: "Mud & Iron" (Badlands Half-Track Escort)
Traversing the Senora Desert in an armored half-track while dodging anti-materiel snipers mounted on wind turbines and low-flying
planes dropping incendiary ordnance.
M44–M48: "The Paleto Deep-Sea Incursion"
Coordinated assault on an Aegis offshore oil platform using Kraken submersibles, mini-gun Mavericks, and hull-breaching thermite
charges to seize cartel bearer bonds.
PART III: SCORCHED EARTH
Missions 49 – 70
Total urban warfare as PMC units, cartel hit squads, and militarized police close the county borders. The crew executes an all-out offensive
to wipe out the leadership of all three organizations simultaneously.
M55: "Skyline Descent" (Triple Penthouse Raid)
Simultaneous coordinated breaches into three high-rises across Downtown within an exact five-minute timer to zero out cartel escrow
accounts.
M62: "Steel Horizon" (Train / Sea / Air Convergence)
Intercepting a high-speed coastal freight train. Ice clears automated flatbed rail turrets, Gohan matches speed in an ocean launch while
jamming signals, and Guess extracts the crew via an Annihilator chopper.
M68–M70: "Blood Brothers" (The Climax)
A three-stage final offensive through the Los Santos River canal, the Maze Bank Tower, and an 18-wheeler running gun battle down
Runway 30L at LSIA, ending with a three-man holdout inside a grounded cargo plane.
