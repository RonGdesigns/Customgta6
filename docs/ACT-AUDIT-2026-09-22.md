# Act audit and crew AI, September 22, 2026

Ron asked for an audit of Acts I, II and III, each on its own, with a close look at Act III
("missions not spawning in the right point, some not starting"). He also asked for M03's men to
stand on the upper yard. During the same session he reported three problems with the brothers:

- They stand still once their job is done.
- They walk to the car "as if they're not in a rush".
- Riding with him through a police shootout, Gohan did nothing.

Everything below was checked with the harnesses only. **None of it has been played.** Items
marked *survey* or *live* are the ones a test cannot settle.

## Shared fixes

**The crew is released again when a mission ends inside its own update.** `Fail()` cleans the
mission up immediately, but the rest of the update still runs. In BM01 that remainder was
`PreparationOperation`'s support pass, and it took Gohan and Guess back after the release.
Gohan then stood through a police shootout in free roam, held by a mission that no longer
existed. There are three guards against this:

- `Mission.Tick` releases the crew again after an update that ended the mission.
- `ComposedMission` stops after an objective ends the mission.
- `PreparationOperation.OnUpdate` does nothing once the mission is over.

**No stage opens after its predecessor's exit fails the mission.** Two of the audit agents asked
for this independently.

**A brother firing from a seat does a drive-by.** The free-roam controller gave passengers the
vehicle-weapon task, which only works for a mounted gun. It also renewed that order every
second, which restarts it before a shot is fired. A turret seat (`IS_TURRET_SEAT`) still works
its gun. Anyone else leans out, and the order is renewed only for a new target or after
`SeatShotRenewMs`.

**Brothers run.** Every mission order that sends a brother to a seat, a post, a job or a
convoy ride now runs, and sprints beyond `CrewBoarding.SprintBeyond`. A brother already at the
door and getting in is not given a new order that would start him over.

**A brother with nothing to do rejoins the player** (`ComposedMission.UpdateIntent`). A
brother has a job when any of these is true:

- he owns an unfinished objective in the stage;
- he was stationed at a point that is not his start key;
- his role is on its way somewhere, working, taking cover or extracting.

With none of those, a brother who is on foot, out of combat, within `RejoinReach` of the
player and standing still for `RejoinIdleMs` is handed to `CompanionController.Rejoin`. From
there he follows the player, fights with him and gets in his vehicle, even while the mission
holds the rest of the crew.

- Any new role order, boarding order or `TakeControl` takes him back.
- Every `PreparationOperation` mission applies this to any idle brother.
- Other missions apply it only once a brother's own objective is done, because their set
  pieces task brothers directly.
- `[Crew] RejoinWhenIdle = False` turns it off.

**Other shared changes:**

- **Boarding.** `CrewBoarding.Crew(driver)` orders the driver too, and M49 to M51 use it.
- **Seat choice.** A boarding brother takes another seat when his planned one is taken.
- **Seated brothers.** `RoleTrack` leaves a seated or boarding brother alone.
- **`Board`.** It accepts the player in any seat and removes a dead occupant.
- **Order timing.** Response cars and support targets are ordered once, not every cycle.
- **Cover.** A cover point more than `RoleTrack.CoverReach` away is ignored. The brother
  fights where he stands.
