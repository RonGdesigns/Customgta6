PRODUCTION BIBLE & TECHNICAL MOD ARCHITECTURELOS SANTOS: BLOODLINESA 70-Mission Tri-Protagonist Overhaul • Character Revision Edition■ 1. EXECUTIVE CONCEPT & NARRATIVE FOUNDATIONLos Santos: Bloodlines is an action-thriller narrative campaign built within the Grand Theft Auto V engine. The storylinecenters on three Black childhood friends from South Central Davis who left on athletic and academic scholarships fifteenyears prior. Having re-entered the criminal underworld through distinct, high-stakes trades, they inadvertently crash into oneanother during an offshore wetwork operation at Terminal Island. The design discards standard slow escort missions in favor of rapid, multi-vehicle combat operations featuring synchronizedcharacter switching, high-altitude drops, sea-to-land breaches, and full-city pursuits. ■ 2.
