# Chase, shops, crew fleet and hideout update

September 12, 2026. This package includes the previously staged P5b rebuild. Automated verification does not replace a GTA playtest. Installation status is recorded separately in the deployment receipt.

## Changes since the installed placement build

* **Debug hair customization restored:** Debug menu > Appearance > character includes Hair and HairColor again. Normal clothing shops and home wardrobes keep those controls disabled. Use Save looks to keep a debug-authored hairstyle across restarts.

* **M05 Tidal Lock:** a visible flare launched from the dinghy, without the obsolete flare destination marker. The flare remains on screen before the Gohan handoff. Approaching Mateo starts a sustained offshore chase, with several water legs before capture can complete. Guess drives while Gohan is active and follows the water route around the island. Thunderstorm weather and stronger swell apply during the mission and are restored afterward. The capture deadline is five minutes; a stalled target produces an actionable failure instead of an endless objective. The surveyed boat and character starting positions remain intact. The old estimated sandbar was inland and has been moved to water.
* **M06 Clean Sweep:** Ice starts beside Gohan. Once all three are aboard the pickup vehicle, the escape permits either brother to be controlled. The two SWAT road teams approach from nearer alley entrances. Exit-vehicle orders finish before combat orders; distant troopers move back toward the fight, with bounded recovery only when the destination and actor are out of view. Implausibly distant convoy overrides are constrained to the alley area.
* **Ammu-Nation:** choose which brother receives purchases. Buy compatible attachments for his owned guns, refit owned attachments free, and preserve his fitted parts through saves and replacement peds. Barrel/high-end scope upgrades require M11; special ammunition components require M28. A full-ammo purchase fills all owned guns for that brother, grouping shared ammunition pools. Full inventories cost nothing; premium ammunition has a higher price. Armor purchases also apply to the selected brother.
* **Vehicle cosmetic previews:** selecting a part fits a temporary preview. Confirm pays the displayed price; Cancel, B/Back, closing the menu, losing shop access, or leaving the vehicle restores the original build. Insufficient cash at confirmation also restores it. Unpaid previews are excluded from garage and crew-car saves. Paint, indexed parts, wheels, toggle equipment, liveries, extras, tint, plates and lighting use this flow. Repairs remain immediate services.
* **Neon colors:** underglow color is now accessible in the lighting menu, with named presets and adjustable RGB channels. It supports the same free preview and cancellation. A vehicle still needs native neon support; this cannot add neon geometry to unsupported models.
* **Foundry crew fleet:** inside the headquarters, open **Crew car fleet** to buy/select a Granger, Baller, Schafter, Armored Kuruma, Buffalo STX, Jubilee, Nightshark or Insurgent. The Granger is already owned. Other prices are $40,000, $38,000, $80,000, $100,000, $90,000, $110,000 and $120,000 respectively. Purchased fleet choices remain owned and can be selected again free. Everyone must leave the existing crew car before replacement. Each model keeps its own customization; the selected model is used by the crew-car mission factory and its scenes. Mission-specific boats, cargo trucks and aircraft keep their required roles. Drive the selected crew car to Customs to upgrade it normally.
* **Complete crew-car persistence:** wheels, custom paint, neon colors and sides, tint, plate style, tire smoke, extras and toggle equipment now persist along with indexed upgrades. This also covers newer indexed slots absent from the older API enum.
* **Car resale:** the quote includes the vehicle's current fitted parts, turbo and other toggle upgrades, neon kit, reinforced tires, livery, tint and extras. Removing a part removes its resale contribution. The allowance is modest (roughly 25% of standard shop installation prices), rather than accumulating every past purchase. Repainting the same car repeatedly cannot manufacture resale value. The existing limit remains **10 sales per GTA day**.
* **Garage placement:** all ten property entrances were checked against installed collision data. Elgin/Pillbox moves to a visible building approach; Popular Street moves off road collision onto the industrial property. Other buried, elevated or misplaced estimated entrances were corrected. Each has a separate road retrieval point. These are collision-checked approaches, not claimed door-by-door visual surveys; personal surveyed overrides remain authoritative.
* **Foundry access:** the installed Enhanced clubhouse map's internal name ends in `_milo_`; the old request omitted the final underscore. The request now matches the archive. Interior loading also supports a protected streaming move before room registration, while retaining collision/readiness checks and timeout recovery. Existing furniture and weapon-locker entity sets load with the room. The crew-car parking point moves out from under the foundry roof.
* **Civilian severe crashes:** a sufficiently hard frontal impact can throw an ordinary driver through the windshield before fatal injury. Minor impacts and side scrapes do not trigger it. Crew, persistent mission actors, police and the player are excluded. A failed seat exit never kills someone still sitting at the wheel. This runs with the existing vehicle/panel damage settings.

## P5b included in the package

M28's relay work uses a physical laptop/case and distinct response choices. M29 has the tanker loading/custody checks, staggered pursuers and their quarter-distance withdrawal. M30 carries a real case through the canyon, transfers it to the receiver workbench, and verifies fitting before awarding completion. See [the P5b implementation notes](RELAY-AND-FUEL-P5B-2026-09-12.md). P5c is still the next unauthored scene/role package; this update does not add M31-M70 runtime missions.

## Focused playtest

1. At the Foundry entrance, enter on foot between missions with no wanted level. Confirm the room, furniture and weapon locker load; exit and re-enter. If loading fails, retain `Bloodlines.log` with its exact waiting stage.
2. Leave the crew vehicle unoccupied. Buy/select an Insurgent, exit the HQ, customize it, switch back to the Granger, then reselect the Insurgent. Check both builds survive a restart and a mission that uses the crew car.
3. At Customs, preview a spoiler, wheels, custom paint and neon RGB. Cancel each and verify no charge or lingering change. Confirm one, then check its sale quote. Do not sell a car you intend to keep just to test the quote.
4. Shop for Gohan while controlling another brother. Buy an attachment and refill all owned guns. Check the intended recipient after switching and reloading.
5. Check Elgin/Pillbox and Popular Street on foot, then retrieve a car at each. Markers should be visible at the property and retrieval should use the road point. F11 can still refine the exact preferred door.
6. Replay M05: watch the flare, approach Mateo, let Guess drive while you control Gohan, complete the offshore run and take Mateo aboard. Check the rain/waves, route around rocks and restoration after abort/completion.
7. Replay M06: Ice starts with Gohan, SWAT stays near the alley, and either brother is selectable after pickup without emptying the vehicle.

Automated tests cover preview/payment rollback, recipient isolation, attachment persistence, refill-all, fleet ownership and separate builds, civilian crash exclusions, mission stage flow and existing campaign behavior. Collision probes sample route segments and property approaches from installed game archives; they do not simulate boat steering, waves, dynamic traffic or live streaming.
