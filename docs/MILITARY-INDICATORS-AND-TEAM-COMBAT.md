# Military indicators, aftermath, teammates and apartments

This follow-up preserves the six-star timing and player-respawn behavior that passed the latest playtest.

## Changes to test

- Military vehicles have attached map/radar markers alternating red and blue: helicopter, convoy/police dot, and tank. A destroyed unit loses its pursuit marker.
- Destroyed vehicles and dead drivers are no longer immediately deleted. The game can finish explosions, fire, falling-helicopter physics and ragdolls. A dead driver also leaves an intact vehicle behind. No extra explosion is synthesized.
- Aftermath stays owned for sixty seconds, up to two minutes when near the player or visible, with a six-unit retention cap. Expired/overflow aftermath is released to ordinary engine cleanup rather than explicitly deleted. Ending six stars or unloading the mod also releases it. Replacement units still use cooldowns and the existing three-active-unit cap.
- Custom military and independent-life officers are allied with native COP and each other. Friendly attacks are disabled on these custom officers. Native police retain normal reactions to other crimes; this does not force every officer in the world to target only the player.
- Teammates recognize attacks on either brother, choose nearby threats, and replace a stale combat target. Follow mode clears old scan/boarding state. On-foot followers react to threats before optional boarding and drop obsolete convoy assignments after disembarking. Drivers, required mission rides and explicit mission holds retain their roles.
- The Free roam crew menu cycles Independent, Ride Along and Drive Alongside. Ride Along uses available passenger seats and immediately acquires overflow transport when the player's vehicle is full. Drive Alongside requests a separate vehicle. Existing shared rides are not forcibly emptied in motion.
- Overflow drivers follow the actual player vehicle with a continuous vehicle-follow task and a following gap. Getting close no longer switches them to destination-free cruising. When the player gets out, the existing parked reunion behavior resumes.
- Transport acquisition prefers parked/empty cars, allows normal carjacking, and can intercept nearby slow traffic with short, spaced bursts at the non-crew driver before normal vehicle entry. It abandons a failed moving interception after six seconds. Crew/player-occupied and persistent mission vehicles are excluded. Catch-up placement retains the existing off-camera and collision checks.
- Guess has the fastest road-driving profile: 50m/s cruise and 60m/s under pursuit. Ice uses 42/50m/s and Gohan 40/48m/s. These are requested speeds, bounded in practice by the vehicle, road and traffic. All use full driving skill with traffic/obstacle avoidance and lane changes; Guess is more aggressive. Drivers concentrate on driving, leaving gunfire to passengers or the user.
- All three heroes now share a 900-health cap and 100 starting/recovery armor, up from 600 health. Switching preserves each hero's remaining health and armor. Start a fresh deployment to see the higher starting health.
- In free roam, a downed teammate gets a separate forty-five-second recovery timer. If a safe point is unavailable, placement retries every three seconds. Recovery creates the hero on navigable ground at least 100m away, outside the camera, then Follow mode lets them travel back. The old corpse is released to engine cleanup. Player-death separation no longer leaves them down forever, and a vanished body does not cancel recovery. Missions retain scripted failure behavior rather than reviving assigned actors mid-mission.
- Luxury entry waits for its requested IPL and ready collision, tries a room-centre lookup if the doorway query fails, and clears stale room assignment after teleport. A frozen player's zero interior ID can be accepted only when ready collision and the interior at the actual destination agree. A mismatched nonzero interior still blocks entry. The bounded timeout is twelve seconds, with logs identifying the stalled stage.

The apartment change addresses possible lookup/room-association delays. The previous live log establishes repeated seven-second rollbacks but does not identify the failed native check; Guess's suite must be retested in the game. Room-centre probes and floor/IPL mappings were checked against the author's [apartment 1](https://github.com/Bob74/bob74_ipl/blob/master/dlc_executive/apartment1.lua), [apartment 2](https://github.com/Bob74/bob74_ipl/blob/master/dlc_executive/apartment2.lua) and [apartment 3](https://github.com/Bob74/bob74_ipl/blob/master/dlc_executive/apartment3.lua) definitions. Existing LocationBook entry coordinates and user overrides remain in use. The [Cfx relationship reference](https://github.com/citizenfx/natives/blob/master/PED/SetRelationshipBetweenGroups.md) documents the reciprocal group links used for custom police allies.

## Retest

1. Enter Guess's luxury suite or its debug preview. Stay inside for twenty seconds, walk around, exit and re-enter. If it rolls back, the new Apartment log entries should identify IPL, interior, collision or room-association failure.
2. Set companions to Follow and fight police on foot. Check that both Ice and Gohan respond, including when an officer targets one of them. After an enemy dies, check that they engage another attacker. Repeat after they drive over and disembark.
3. In free roam, let a teammate go down. After roughly forty-five seconds, check the recovery notice and their return journey. Die as the player separately: teammates should not appear at your respawn, but downed teammates can later recover on their own timer.
4. At six stars, check all three military icons. Destroy a vehicle and watch the aftermath remain while a replacement eventually arrives. Shoot only a helicopter pilot: the helicopter should continue its physical fall rather than disappear.
5. Choose Ride Along and use a two-seat car with both teammates nearby. One takes the available seat; the other obtains a separate car and follows across several blocks. Slow down and speed up, then stop and get out. Try Drive Alongside with a four-seat car and confirm separate transport is chosen. Switch to a passenger to compare Guess's driving with Ice's and Gohan's.

Validation uses native stand-ins: compilation of 93 production sources, 165 behavioral/recovery checks, 315 story/runtime checks, mission lint and location validation. These checks do not establish real game physics, AI targeting or interior streaming. Install replaces only Bloodlines.dll and preserves saves, wardrobe, configuration and mission data.
