# Act One playtest, September 22, 2026

Ron played M01 to M05 on the September 22 build and reported five things. This is what
each turned out to be and what changed. Nothing here has been played since the change.

## 1. The scene camera did not come back to the character

**Cause.** A scene hands the view back by *sliding* into the player's camera (September 15),
which keeps the scene camera rendering for about a second. Most missions start a staged
scene straight after the briefing. Today's log shows M01's arrival and call 0.4 s apart, M02's
briefing and stash 0.27 s apart, M03's briefing and split 1.1 s apart, and M05's account and
aftermath 0.02 s apart. The second scene asked what camera was rendering, got the first
scene's sliding camera, took it for another system's view, and restored it when it ended.
The view then sat on a dead shot. The host's clean-up of old cameras was the last step of
the tick, and a running scene returns before it, so that camera stayed alive long enough to
be restored.

A static scan finds 69 of 79 missions open a scene in their setup, straight after the
briefing. That is why this is fixed once in `CutsceneDirector`, not per mission:

* A new scene retires any camera still sliding home before it looks at what is rendering,
  and never treats this director's own camera as a view to restore.
* A second hand-back retires the first camera instead of orphaning it.
* If a scene camera is somehow still rendering when its slide should be over, the view is
  handed back to gameplay before the camera is deleted, with a log warning.
* The host retires cameras first in the tick, ahead of every early return.
* The eased `RENDER_SCRIPT_CAMS` passes all six arguments, as the game's own scripts do.
* The `CONTROL [scene ended]` log line now says who has the view: `camera=gameplay`,
  `scene`, `handing-back` or `script:<handle>`.

The "finish screen" in M01 and M05 is the aftermath scene after MISSION PASSED. M05's
matches exactly: its account scene was skipped and the aftermath started 23 ms later. M01's
aftermath had no scene directly before it in today's log, so if M01's finish sticks again,
the new `camera=` field on that line will say what held the view.

## 2. M05: the boarding could not be done on a moving boat

"Take him aboard" is Gohan's, and it required the dinghy under 1 m/s within 20 m of Mateo.
With the ocean scaled up for the storm, swell alone moves an idle dinghy past 1 m/s, and the
hold restarted every time it did. Once the player switched to Gohan, nobody was steering.

* Mateo's stopped boat drops anchor when the boarding opens.
* While the player is not at the wheel, Guess brings the dinghy back alongside, within 12 m,
  then cuts the engine and anchors. The approach is re-issued on a six-second refresh only,
  never every frame. If the player drives, the dinghy is never anchored under him.
* A boat counts as stopped under 3.5 m/s. The reach is measured flat, so a wave lifting one
  boat is not drifting apart. A bigger wave **pauses** a hold in progress for up to 2.5 s,
  and says so, instead of throwing the work away. This applies to any hold done from a boat.
* Both anchors are lifted when Mateo's account ends, and again at cleanup.

## 3. M05: Ice's ability, explained

When Ice takes the cliff, two notices explain Thermal Pulse and how to use it: the
configured key (Caps Lock by default), or both sticks (L3 + R3) on a controller. They also
give how long it runs and a reminder to scope in.

Thermal Pulse outlined bodies only within 60 m. Ice's perch is about 270 m above the
generator crew, so on this job it marked nobody. **Hostiles are now marked out to 300 m**,
with the marker growing with range so it reads through a scope. Civilians and the crew are
still only outlined within 60 m. This is a change to the ability, made so the explanation is
true on the mission it is given on. Say if it should apply to M05 only.

The fleet service's hardened cars sat at the dealer's price for the same model. They now
carry roughly a third on top. The plain Baller and Schafter are unchanged, and the Granger
is still free.

## 5. The mission surveyor

See `docs/MISSION-PLACEMENT-EDITOR.md`. The survey page is reordered so Save is the second
row, and **Y** saves from any row. **Add enemies, vehicles, props** places extra groups of
men, a vehicle with men in it, or a prop. The stand-in shows the actual model, **Y** loads
the next one and **X** drops it. Additions save to `Bloodlines.Additions.tsv` the moment they
are dropped and spawn the next time the mission starts.

"Place more markers" is read here as more placed points, each drawn where it will spawn.
If it meant new objective markers a mission would send the player to, that needs mission
code per marker and was not attempted.
