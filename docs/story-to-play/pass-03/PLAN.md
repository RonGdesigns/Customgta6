# Pass 03 — M54-M60 Urban Operations and Siege of Davis

## M54 — The Pillbox Redoubt

Story purpose: create a **temporary operations/observation room** for the downtown offensive, not silently turn another penthouse into the brothers' permanent home.

Playable treatment:
- Begin outside a verified building/entrance with a known safe return marker.
- Ron/Guess restores or bypasses the supported access route and verifies the exit.
- Ice secures the usable balcony/lookout and identifies Maze Bank/approach sightlines.
- Gohan installs/connects the transmitter/uplink and verifies it actually functions.
- The trio inspects the room together only after each physical task succeeds.
- If the intended interior is unavailable or fails streaming, setup must refuse or use an explicitly approved alternative; a safehouse flag is not proof of an installed interior.
- Consequence: three escrow terminals require overlapping access windows; each brother will hold one.

## M55 — Skyline Descent: synchronized three-site prototype

This is the key switching prototype for the later campaign.

### Required structure

1. Each brother physically reaches a different verified staging site.
2. The shared five-minute operation clock starts only after all three are ready.
3. Player switching is meaningful: the player performs each brother's decisive action.
4. Inactive brothers maintain already-established work/connection and defend themselves; `RoleState.Working` standing still is not sufficient for a real terminal operation.
5. Switching must not reset the clock, heal/refill, respawn/recreate a brother, teleport credentials or silently complete another site.
6. The operation is successful only when all three required terminal results are simultaneously valid.
7. Hitting the third node is not an automatic mission end; each brother needs a supported exit/regroup path.

### Financial meaning

M55 **constrains/freezes the escrow routes**. It does not pay out the $500M; master authorization remains at Maze Bank and later transfer logic must keep evidence, bearer instruments and escrow money separate.

### Dialogue timing

Countdown lines such as a remaining-seconds call must be triggered by the actual shared timer, not played whenever a scripted objective happens to finish. Lines claiming all nodes are compromised require all three node states to be true.

## M60 — Siege of Davis

Story purpose: protect people from retaliation caused by the crew's actions; **resident safety is the objective**, not simply enemy body count.

Proposed roles:
- Ron moves residents through the crossing, manages vehicles/pickup and keeps an exit usable.
- Ice suppresses specific threats blocking evacuation and relocates as routes change.
- Gohan identifies flanks and performs a supported diversion/disable action while maintaining the evacuation picture.

Mission structure:
1. Arrive visibly together and establish which residents/routes need protection.
2. Begin evacuation before the first full wave.
3. Three bounded enemy responses react to the player and route state.
4. Use mission-owned obstacles/disable actions that do not destroy civilian-critical infrastructure unless explicitly approved.
5. Killing all attackers is not sufficient: required residents must reach safety and the brothers must be accounted for.
6. Quiet aftermath: residents emerge, aid/water appears, the brothers acknowledge the damage and identify the Terminal wreck lead for M61.

Older lines about burning muscle-car roadblocks or detonating a gas main conflict with the resident-protection framing and require explicit approval/support before implementation.
