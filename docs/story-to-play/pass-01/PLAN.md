# Pass 01 — Full Campaign Alignment and Paleto Proposal

## Scope

Pass 01 aligned the prologue, all 70 main missions, all nine solo missions and the ending against the current repository story, prior owner decisions and the campaign novel. Its purpose is to make every mission understandable through gameplay: why each brother is present, what his role is, what physically changes, how the result reaches the next job, and which source conflicts must not silently overwrite approved gameplay.

At the reviewed baseline, gameplay is implemented through M43 and SM06. Pass 01 does not claim later missions are playable.

## Campaign-wide rules

1. **Context before control.** Before the player receives control, the mission must establish the reason for the job and why the active brother is in his position. M04 is the reference problem: Ron cannot simply be sitting in a car with no explanation; the player must understand that he is covering Miller's escape route.
2. **Use scenes only when something must be seen.** Travel/radio can carry information without stopping gameplay. A scene is justified when location, object custody, a person arriving/leaving, a route, or a changed world state must be visible.
3. **Physical result before narrative claim.** A case must be attached/loaded, a brother must board, a vehicle must arrive, and evidence must be held before dialogue says it happened.
4. **Consequence before next job.** Each mission's exit identifies where the brothers, evidence, cargo and transport actually end up and why the next mission follows.
5. **Watch and skip reach the same required result; cancel/failure is not success.**
6. **Approved gameplay outranks conflicting older prose.** Example: M25 keeps the approved parachute-to-Ron's-boat ending and required 100-meter departure instead of reverting to the novel's climb-down/buggy version.
7. **Book chapter numbers do not rename missions.** Later novel chapters shift relative to mission IDs; map by event/title.

## Character alignment

- Ron/Guess is the starting perspective, driver/mechanical problem-solver and dependable connector. He often makes the exit work, but he is not commander over Ice or Gohan.
- Ice is tactical overwatch and direct-force specialist. His discipline must not become constant commander dialogue over equal brothers.
- Gohan is the analytical/digital breaker. His phone/device habits are character texture, not a story test of whether he belongs.
- All three must have real work when present. Inactive heroes do not exist merely to stand in a marker or materialize at an extraction.

## M44-M48 story purpose

M31-M43 prepares a single offshore assault: perimeter/EMP work, Ramos rescue, armored support, survey, aircraft smoke, seismic stock, cable cut, extraction launches, Bradley's keycard, submarine delivery and Paleto staging. M44-M48 must pay those preparations off visibly.

### Proposed continuous physical story

**M44 — Sub-Surface**
- Gohan starts in the prepared Kraken and approaches real underwater defense/structural targets.
- The EMP/sabotage must visibly change the sea-defense response.
- Charges attach to reachable physical targets; no markers floating in empty water.
- Ron and Ice retain real staging assignments rather than teleporting to the next section.

**M45 — Helipad Breach**
- Ron flies the actual insertion vehicle to a footprint-checked landing/drop area.
- Ice disembarks normally and secures the required access.
- Gohan surfaces, leaves the submarine and follows a real supported route to the secure-area threshold with Bradley's credential.
- A flyby or cut cannot substitute for the necessary transfer.

**M46 — Vault Crack**
- Ice and Gohan use the acquired access and perform separate physical breach/security tasks.
- The political ledger/evidence is copied and retained as evidence.
- Any carried bearer bonds/cash are separate from the vault's face value and later escrow authorization.
- Ron prepares the real extraction rather than disappearing from the operation.

**M47 — Collapse / Pickup**
- Demolition cannot trigger until the crew confirms a usable exit.
- The recommended continuity has Ron transition from insertion helicopter to the prepared launch and perform the pickup while Ice and Gohan jump.
- Full tilting/sinking-rig spectacle remains unproven; use controlled destruction unless the physical set can support more.
- Required evidence stays with a known holder through the jump/pickup.

**M48 — Road Back South**
- The same extraction craft reaches shore.
- Crew/evidence/cargo transfer physically into the prepared road vehicle.
- The crew breaks the outer coastal cordon and reaches a defined southbound stop.
- Chumash remains a separate second line in M49; M48 must not claim they are fully back inside Los Santos.
