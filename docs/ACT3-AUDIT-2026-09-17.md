# Act III audit, September 17, 2026

Ron asked for Act III's placements and start points checked, and for the missions to be
confirmed running the way they are supposed to. This is what was found, what was fixed, and
what is still a live question.

## The finding that matters most

**No Act III mission had ever been driven through its stages, even in the harness.** Every
main mission from M49 derives from `PreparationOperation`, and the campaign flow walker
aborted every `PreparationOperation` before its first stage on the grounds that M31 to M40
had dedicated walkthroughs. M49 to M70 did not. Four of the walker's own Act III hooks
(M65, M66, M67, M68) were dead code behind that skip.

The walker now drives every main mission from M49, with the beats it needs produced rather
than faked: M49's spotlight towers destroyed, M52's Harrison walked clear and then taken,
M54's back seats filled, M65's Vance killed only on the beat that asks for it. **All 22 walk
to Pass through real objective updates.** That is the harness's verdict, not a playtest's.

## A brother placed where the player is not

M59 creates Ice on the Vinewood sign 515 m from the transmitter yard where Gohan starts. M55
stations two brothers in penthouses 450 m from the first. Collision streams around the
player and the focus, not around every ped, so such a man stands where the world has not
been built. `Core/FarPlacement` asks the engine to keep collision loaded around him
(`SET_ENTITY_LOAD_COLLISION_FLAG`) and requests it now; the roster applies it to any brother
deployed more than 150 m from the lead, and `Station` applies it to one moved that far.

It never freezes him: the switch machinery already waits on collision before handing him
over, and a freeze a scene captured and restored would be a brother nobody ever thawed.
**Whether he is standing where he should be when the player switches to him is a road test.**

## Start points and placements, mechanically

`validate_locations` puts every Act III key in the zone its district text names. The kinds
are coherent with the geometry: roof and plaza keys are declared fixed surfaces, water keys
resolve through `MarineSites`, air keys go through `LaunchAirborne`, the metro and the drain
are measured once from one datum. The outliers by distance from the start (M54's roof at
1.8 km, M66's landing at 1.2 km, M67's airport at 1.6 km, SM09's berth at 1.4 km) are all
travel legs, not spawns.

None of it is surveyed. Every Act III key is still an estimate, and the sweep and the
readout built the day before are how that changes.

## Found on the way, outside Act III

**Buying a car on the phone showed no stats at all.** `VehicleSpecs.Of` requested the model
and released it in the same call, every frame. A request is not a load - the engine streams
over the frames that follow - and the release before it landed canceled it. Nothing was
ever readable and every car row said "reading ratings" for as long as Ron looked at it. The
request stays open now until the ratings are read, the natives are tried by hash first, and
the world menu's rows exist before the model answers and fill in after. The harness stand-in
streamed synchronously, which is why the earlier checks passed; it streams like the engine
now and the reproduction is in the suite.

**The host constructor threw on the previous build** (PR #93): the shop's stage lookup was
wired before the shop existed. Only `DevTools` started. A text check now refuses any field
dereferenced in the constructor before the line that builds it.

Ron also mentioned a silhouette on the phone's car page. There is none in the code and there
never was; it is a feature to build, not one that broke.
